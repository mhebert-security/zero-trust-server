about

Nursing → computer science → cybersecurity: five years of clinical work taught me to expect failure and design around it, which is what safety-critical systems — and security — actually demand.

I carried a 3.9 GPA through computer science and am now charting a master's in cybersecurity, pointed at OT/ICS defense. The code I write tends to be small, explicable, and hostile to dependencies, for the same reason the medication room was locked twice.

Based in Toronto. I write in public as I go — poorly documented ideas, honestly labeled — on the writing page.
