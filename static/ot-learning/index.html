OT/ICS Security: Learning in Public

From Bedside to Substation: Learning ICS/OT Security from First Principles. This series documents building OT security competency from scratch, starting with the mental model and ending with a working detection lab. Each article pairs with a technical artifact on GitHub. Nurse to contractor to security practitioner.

Module 1 · The Mental Model

Why OT security is not just IT security with hard hats, and the incidents that defined the field.

Module 2 · Protocol Literacy

How Modbus, EtherNet/IP, DNP3, and IEC 61850 move control traffic and where they lack authentication.

Module 3 · Threat Detection Applied to OT

Extending MITRE ATT&CK into the plant floor and standing up traffic visibility.

Module 4 · Standards and Compliance

IEC 62443 and NERC CIP, translated from jargon into the controls they actually require.

Module 5 · Medical Device Security

The hospital network from the bedside, where infusion pumps and DICOM imaging carry patient data.

Module 6 · Capstone

A full OT detection lab on a real budget, with the architecture and the cost written out.

The infusion pump threat model and the OT detection lab repo are the two artifacts I expect practitioners to find most useful. Both are linked from their respective articles when complete.
